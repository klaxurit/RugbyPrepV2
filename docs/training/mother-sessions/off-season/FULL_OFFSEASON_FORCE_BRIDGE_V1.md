# FULL_OFFSEASON_FORCE_BRIDGE_V1

- `status`: validated
- `version`: V1
- `cycle`: off_season
- `session_type`: full
- `target_level`: performance
- `target_position_group`: front_row + back_three (common base with light accents)
- `equipment`: full_gym
- `target_duration`: 55-65 min

## Goal
- Complete the off-season force-bridge week with a full-body session that reinforces force without carrying hypertrophy fatigue into pre-season.
- Complement the Lower and Upper Force-Bridge sessions without simply repeating their anchor lifts.
- Keep one hinge-led full-body anchor, one upper push/pull support block, one unilateral/trunk block, and one small tissue continuity finish.

## Session Identity
- This is a force-bridge full-body session, not a hypertrophy full-body day and not yet a pre-season power session.
- Rugby-specific through useful hinge, upper support, unilateral lower support, trunk control, and tissue continuity.
- Do not turn this into a heavy fourth-day challenge or a hidden conditioning circuit.

## Warm-Up
Warm-up is stored as a collapsible recommendation rather than a mandatory visible block.

### Recommended warm-up
- `ankle rocks` `1x8/side`
- `adductor rock-back` `1x8/side`
- `thoracic rotation` `1x6/side`
- `scap push-up` `1x8`
- `2 progressive ramp-up sets` on the first loaded movement

### Notes
- Keep this practical and short.
- The player should feel ready for denser work, not like they are about to max out.
- If they already have a good full-body warm-up, they can keep it.

## Visible Blocks

### Block 1 - Main Full-Body Hinge Bridge
- Format: `4 work sets`, `2-3 min` rest between sets
- Exercise A: `Trap Bar Deadlift` `4x4-5`
- Coaching notes:
  - Keep the trap bar around `RPE 6-8`.
  - This is the anchor force-bridge lift of the session.
  - The goal is to feel denser and stronger than in Hypertrophy, not to test max strength.
  - Fallback options:
    - A: `Barbell Romanian Deadlift`

### Block 2 - Upper Push / Pull Support
- Format: `3 rounds`, `90-120s` rest after the pair
- Exercise A: `DB Incline Bench Press` `3x6-8`
- Exercise B: `Single-Arm DB Row` `3x6-8/side`
- Coaching notes:
  - This block keeps useful upper support volume while total week volume narrows.
  - Both movements should stay clean, controlled, and clearly below failure.
  - This is support, not the center of the session.
  - Fallback options:
    - A: `Neutral-Grip DB Bench Press`
    - B: `Chest-Supported Row`

### Block 3 - Unilateral / Trunk Support
- Format: `3 rounds`, `75-90s` rest after the pair
- Exercise A: `Reverse Lunge` `3x6/side`
- Exercise B: `Pallof Press Hold` `3x15-20s/side`
- Coaching notes:
  - Keep one more unilateral lower exposure in the week without turning this into another lower day.
  - The lunge should feel stable and forceful, not fatigue-heavy.
  - Pallof should stay crisp and posture-driven.
  - Fallback options:
    - A: `Split Squat`
    - B: `Side Plank`

### Block 4 - Groin / Lower-Leg Continuity
- Format: `2 rounds`, minimal rest
- Exercise A: `Copenhagen Hold` `2x20-30s/side`
- Exercise B: `Single-Leg Calf Raise` `2x10/side`
- Exercise C: `Wall Tibialis Raise` `2x12-15`
- Coaching notes:
  - Keep tissue continuity in place as force rises and volume narrows.
  - This block should feel tidy and useful, not like extra punishment.

## Progression Rules
- `S9`: establish denser reference loads after Hypertrophy.
- `S10`: add load only if the player is recovering well and the session still feels cleaner than the previous phase.
- Progress force expression, not exhaustion.
- If fatigue rises, reduce Block 4 first, then reduce one round from Block 3.
- Keep Blocks 1 and 2 as the structural priorities of the session.

## Position Accent
- This session is still mostly common.
- `Front_row` accent:
  - slightly more bracing and posture through trap bar and lunge
  - slightly more interest in simple trunk support
- `Back_three` accent:
  - slightly more fluidity and range in lunge and lower-leg work
  - slightly more attention to movement quality than brute loading
- The skeleton remains identical for both groups.

## Injury Substitutions

### Shoulder pain
- Remove:
  - `DB Incline Bench Press`
  - unsupported row only if support position is aggravating
- Replace with:
  - `Neutral-Grip DB Bench Press`
  - `Chest-Supported Row`
- Rehab finisher:
  - `band external rotation`
  - `serratus reach`

### Knee pain
- Remove:
  - `Reverse Lunge`
  - `Copenhagen Hold` only if clearly aggravating
- Replace with:
  - `Split Squat` reduced range
  - `Supine Adductor Squeeze`
- Rehab finisher:
  - controlled knee-friendly squat pattern
  - light terminal knee extension if needed

### Low back pain
- Remove:
  - `Trap Bar Deadlift`
  - unsupported row only if posture breaks down
  - `Pallof Press Hold` if trunk demand is aggravating
- Replace with:
  - `Barbell Romanian Deadlift` lighter
  - `Chest-Supported Row`
  - `Side Plank`
- Rehab finisher:
  - breathing and trunk stiffness work

## Coaching Warnings
- This session should complete the bridge into pre-season, not leave the player carrying residual fatigue.
- Do not let the trap bar become a max-strength ego lift.
- Do not let the support blocks drift back toward hypertrophy fluff.
- The bridge works only if the player arrives stronger, cleaner, and fresher into pre-season.
