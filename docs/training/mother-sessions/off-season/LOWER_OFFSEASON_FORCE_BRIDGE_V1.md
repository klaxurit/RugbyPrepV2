# LOWER_OFFSEASON_FORCE_BRIDGE_V1

- `status`: validated
- `version`: V1
- `cycle`: off_season
- `session_type`: lower
- `target_level`: performance
- `target_position_group`: front_row + back_three (common base with light accents)
- `equipment`: full_gym
- `target_duration`: 50-60 min

## Goal
- Densify lower-body force after the hypertrophy block without jumping straight into pre-season structure.
- Reduce volume from Hypertrophy while keeping squat, hinge, unilateral support, and groin/lower-leg continuity.
- Prepare the player to enter pre-season with more usable force and better loading tolerance.

## Session Identity
- This is a force-bridge lower session, not a hypertrophy accumulation day and not yet a pre-season force session.
- Rugby-specific through useful squat and hinge loading, simple unilateral support, and tissue continuity.
- Do not turn this into a heavy max-strength day or an early contrast session.

## Warm-Up
Warm-up is stored as a collapsible recommendation rather than a mandatory visible block.

### Recommended warm-up
- `ankle rocks` `1x8/side`
- `adductor rock-back` `1x8/side`
- `glute bridge` `1x8`
- `bodyweight split squat` `1x6/side`
- `2 progressive ramp-up sets` on the first loaded movement

### Notes
- Keep this practical and short.
- The player should feel ready for heavier work than Hypertrophy, but not like a max-effort day.
- If they already have a good lower warm-up, they can keep it.

## Visible Blocks

### Block 1 - Main Lower Force Bridge
- Format: `4 work sets`, `2-3 min` rest between sets
- Exercise A: `Back Squat` `4x5`
- Coaching notes:
  - Keep the squat around `RPE 6-8`.
  - This is the anchor force-bridge lift of the session.
  - The goal is to feel stronger and denser than in Hypertrophy, not to chase grinders.
  - Fallback options:
    - A: `Front Squat`
    - B: `Box Squat`

### Block 2 - Hinge / Unilateral Support Pair
- Format: `3 rounds`, `90-120s` rest after the pair
- Exercise A: `Barbell Romanian Deadlift` `3x6`
- Exercise B: `Rear-Foot Elevated Split Squat` `3x6/side`
- Coaching notes:
  - This block keeps useful posterior-chain and unilateral force support while volume comes down from Hypertrophy.
  - The hinge should feel strong and crisp, not exhausting.
  - The unilateral work should stay controlled and balanced.
  - Fallback options:
    - A: `DB Romanian Deadlift`
    - B: `Reverse Lunge`

### Block 3 - Hamstring / Groin Support
- Format: `2-3 rounds`, `60-75s` rest after the pair
- Exercise A: `Lying Leg Curl` `2-3x8-10`
- Exercise B: `Copenhagen Hold` `2x20-30s/side`
- Coaching notes:
  - This block keeps hamstring and groin support in place while total session volume drops.
  - Keep the work clean and submaximal.
  - This is support, not the center of the session.
  - Fallback options:
    - A: `Seated Leg Curl`
    - B: `Supine Adductor Squeeze`

### Block 4 - Lower-Leg Continuity
- Format: `2 rounds`, minimal rest
- Exercise A: `Seated Calf Raise` `2x10-12`
- Exercise B: `Wall Tibialis Raise` `2x12-15`
- Coaching notes:
  - Keep lower-leg tissues in the plan as force rises and volume narrows.
  - This block should feel tidy and useful, not like extra punishment.

## Progression Rules
- `S9`: establish denser reference loads after Hypertrophy.
- `S10`: add load only if bar speed and posture stay clearly better than the previous phase.
- Progress force expression, not fatigue.
- If fatigue rises, reduce Block 4 first, then reduce one round from Block 3.
- Keep Blocks 1 and 2 as the structural priorities of the session.

## Position Accent
- This session is still mostly common.
- `Front_row` accent:
  - slightly more bracing and patience under the squat
  - slightly more force bias in the hinge
- `Back_three` accent:
  - slightly more fluid movement quality on unilateral work
  - slightly more attention to lower-leg quality
- The skeleton remains identical for both groups.

## Injury Substitutions

### Shoulder pain
- Remove:
  - `Back Squat` only if bar position is aggravating
- Replace with:
  - `Front Squat`
  - `Box Squat`
- Rehab finisher:
  - `band external rotation`
  - `serratus reach`

### Knee pain
- Remove:
  - `Back Squat`
  - `Rear-Foot Elevated Split Squat`
- Replace with:
  - `Box Squat` light-moderate
  - `Reverse Lunge` reduced range
- Rehab finisher:
  - controlled knee-friendly squat pattern
  - light terminal knee extension if needed

### Low back pain
- Remove:
  - `Barbell Romanian Deadlift`
  - `Back Squat` only if bracing cannot stay clean
- Replace with:
  - `DB Romanian Deadlift`
  - `Hack Squat`
- Rehab finisher:
  - breathing and trunk stiffness work

## Coaching Warnings
- This session should feel denser than Hypertrophy, not more brutal.
- Do not let the lower volume trick the player into chasing maxes.
- Do not let the support blocks drift back toward hypertrophy fluff.
- The bridge works only if the player arrives fresher, not trashed, into pre-season.
